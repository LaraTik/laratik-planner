# Just Halal + Dr Reem Reda — 16-week bilingual content plan

**Planning window:** 16 weeks (four months from the chosen launch week)  
**Cadence:** three feed posts per brand per week; optional fourth post when there is a real offer, timely question, or community moment  
**Primary goals:** brand awareness, qualified page follows, mobile-app downloads, repeat orders, and trust  
**Formats:** short vertical video, carousel, static proof post, story sequence, and app push/email reuse

This is a reusable editorial plan, not a promise about current stock, delivery
coverage, prices, clinical outcomes, or halal certification. Replace bracketed
fields before publishing and obtain brand/legal/clinical review where marked.

## Operating model

### Weekly rhythm

| Day             | Just Halal                                         | Dr Reem Reda                                            | Primary job             |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Monday          | Reel: useful grocery or basket insight             | Reel: one practical psychology idea                     | Reach and follows       |
| Wednesday       | Carousel: proof, education, or meal solution       | Carousel: saveable framework or myth correction         | Saves and shares        |
| Friday          | Reel: app, delivery, community, or seasonal moment | Reel: question, reflection, or audience prompt          | Conversion and comments |
| Optional Sunday | Offer, stock arrival, UGC, or local event          | Q&A, live reminder, or response to a recurring question | Timely demand           |

Run each brand in its own voice and account. Cross-promote only when the
audience overlap is real; do not mix supermarket promotions into a clinical
psychology post or imply that shopping solves a mental-health concern.

### Content guardrails

- Just Halal: use `[product]`, `[price]`, `[delivery area]`, `[delivery window]`, and `[app link]` placeholders until operations confirms them. Never claim “certified,” “available,” “same-day,” or “best price” without evidence.
- Dr Reem Reda: use educational language such as “may help,” “one idea to try,” and “consider speaking with a qualified professional.” Do not diagnose, promise treatment, or turn a viewer’s comment into a public case study without consent.
- Arabic copy must receive native editorial review. Preserve named products, URLs, handles, and prices in LTR/bidi-safe elements.
- Every post has one dominant CTA. Secondary actions belong in the caption or first comment.
- Use official vector icons and real product/creator footage. Do not use emoji as structural UI icons.
- Keep hooks readable at 375px, captions skimmable, subtitles on every spoken Reel, and reduced-motion alternatives for animated carousels.

## Reusable scripts

### JH-01 — “One basket, one solution” Reel (25–35 seconds)

**Purpose:** make Just Halal useful before asking for an install.

1. **0–2s hook:** “Need [meal/occasion] without a second shop?” / «تحتاج مكونات [الوجبة/المناسبة] من دون جولة تسوق ثانية؟»
2. **2–8s:** show three to five real products; overlay the meal/occasion and confirmed availability.
3. **8–20s:** build the basket in the app or show the aisle order: protein, staple, fresh item, finishing touch.
4. **20–28s:** show delivery area/time placeholder and the total only after it is confirmed.
5. **28–35s CTA:** “Follow for practical halal shopping ideas. Download the app when you’re ready to order.” / «تابعنا لأفكار تسوق حلال عملية. حمّل التطبيق عندما تكون مستعدًا للطلب.»

**Caption:** “A simple basket for [occasion]. Save this list, then check today’s stock in the Just Halal app. [Delivery disclaimer].”  
**Arabic caption:** «سلة بسيطة لـ[المناسبة]. احفظ القائمة ثم تحقق من المنتجات المتاحة اليوم في تطبيق Just Halal. [تنبيه التوصيل].»

### JH-02 — “Trust you can check” carousel (7 slides)

1. Cover: “What should you check before adding a product?” / «ما الذي تتحقق منه قبل إضافة المنتج؟»
2. Product name and category.
3. Ingredients/allergen panel.
4. Certification or supplier information **only when confirmed**.
5. Pack size and value comparison.
6. Delivery/storage note.
7. CTA: “Save this checklist and follow for clearer halal shopping.” / «احفظ قائمة التحقق وتابعنا لتسوق حلال أوضح.»

**Design:** one idea per slide, high contrast, no tiny screenshots, source/last-reviewed date in the final slide.

### JH-03 — App-download Reel (15–25 seconds)

**Hook:** “Your next halal shop can start before you leave home.” / «يمكن أن يبدأ تسوقك الحلال القادم قبل أن تغادر المنزل.»

Show: open app → search/category → add one item → review substitution/delivery note → checkout screen without exposing personal data.  
**CTA:** “Download from [App Store/Google Play link]. Follow for weekly basket ideas.” / «حمّل التطبيق من [الرابط]. تابعنا لأفكار سلال أسبوعية.»

### JH-04 — Community question Reel (20–30 seconds)

**Hook:** “What is hardest to find in your local halal shop?” / «ما أكثر شيء يصعب العثور عليه في متجر الحلال القريب منك؟»

Show three answer options as on-screen text, invite comments, promise only to review demand—not to guarantee stock. Reply to useful comments with permission and hide personal order details.

### RR-01 — Practical psychology Reel (30–45 seconds)

**Purpose:** teach one small, non-diagnostic idea.

1. **Hook:** “If your mind keeps replaying the same thought, try this 30-second reset.” / «إذا ظل عقلك يعيد الفكرة نفسها، جرّب إعادة الضبط هذه لمدة 30 ثانية.»
2. Name the experience without diagnosing: “Some people notice…” / «يلاحظ بعض الناس…»
3. Give one step: name the thought, name the feeling, choose one next action.
4. Add boundary: “This is educational, not a diagnosis or a substitute for care.” / «هذا محتوى تثقيفي وليس تشخيصًا أو بديلًا عن الرعاية.»
5. CTA: “Follow for grounded psychology in Arabic and English.” / «تابع للمزيد من علم النفس العملي بالعربية والإنجليزية.»

### RR-02 — Saveable framework carousel (6 slides)

1. Cover: “Pause before you react.” / «توقّف لحظة قبل أن تتفاعل.»
2. What happened? / ماذا حدث؟
3. What story did my mind add? / ما القصة التي أضافها عقلي؟
4. What feeling is present? / ما الشعور الموجود؟
5. What response matches my values? / ما الاستجابة التي تنسجم مع قيمي؟
6. Footer: “If this feels overwhelming or persistent, seek qualified support.” / «إذا كان الأمر مرهقًا أو مستمرًا، اطلب دعمًا من مختص مؤهل.»

### RR-03 — Myth and nuance Reel (30–45 seconds)

**Hook:** “A popular psychology sentence that needs more nuance…” / «عبارة شائعة في علم النفس تحتاج إلى مزيد من التوضيح…»

State the popular claim, explain the limitation in plain language, offer a safer alternative, and invite a respectful question. Never name or diagnose a commenter.  
**CTA:** “Save this for the next time you hear the claim; follow for more nuance.” / «احفظها عندما تسمع العبارة مرة أخرى، وتابع للمزيد من التوضيح.»

### RR-04 — Question-led community post

**Prompt:** “What would make a difficult day 5% easier?” / «ما الشيء الذي قد يجعل اليوم الصعب أسهل بنسبة 5٪؟»

Offer three non-clinical examples (water, a short walk, one message to a trusted person), invite optional answers, and state that viewers should avoid sharing identifying or crisis details in public comments. Use the approved support-resource footer for the account’s country.

## Caption and publishing checklist

Before scheduling each post:

1. Confirm product, stock, price, delivery, link, and location claims.
2. Confirm the English hook and native Arabic version; check RTL layout at 375px.
3. Add subtitles, descriptive alt text, and a source/last-reviewed note for factual posts.
4. Verify the one CTA and use a tagged link for app downloads.
5. For psychology content, run clinical/editorial review and attach the approved support-resource footer.
6. Record format, pillar, language, hook, CTA, source, planned date, and owner in Planner.
7. After 48–72 hours, record reach, watch time, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, link clicks, follows, and app installs/orders where available. Do not infer missing metrics.

## Monthly review and experiments

- **Month 1:** test practical hooks versus question hooks; establish baseline by language and format.
- **Month 2:** test carousels with checklist endings versus direct CTAs; promote only posts with confirmed operational capacity.
- **Month 3:** test app-demo length (15s versus 25s) and Arabic-first versus English-first opening frames.
- **Month 4:** keep the top two formats, retire the bottom format, and create the next 16-week plan from observed evidence rather than vanity reach.

Use Planner Research to save winning source observations, write a reviewed
teardown, and create an editable brief. Do not copy a competitor’s footage,
caption, or distinctive creative expression; adapt the underlying customer
problem, evidence, and format idea into an original Just Halal or Dr Reem post.
