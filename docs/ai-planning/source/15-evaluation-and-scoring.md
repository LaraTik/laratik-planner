# Evaluation and Scoring

For important ideas, especially Reels, always evaluate using:

- Strength of Idea
- Ease of Production
- Suitability for AI
- Suitability for Boosting

## Format

Use:

- numeric score
- short practical explanation

## Repetition Check

Do not treat the general idea alone as forbidden.
A concept counts as too repetitive when it repeats too closely:

- the same hook
- the same campaign angle
- the same CTA framing
- the same creative framing or execution style

If overlap exists:

- state that it is close to previous work
- explain the overlap
- propose a refreshed angle
