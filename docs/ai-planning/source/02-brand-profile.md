# Brand Profile Framework

## Core Brand Info

Collect:

- Client name
- Business type
- Industry
- City / country / service area
- Short business description
- Main products or services
- Main audience
- Current platforms
- Brand tone
- Language context
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Current offers
- Competitors if known

## Marketing Operations Info

Collect:

- Monthly business goals
- Current account status
- KPI priorities
- Budget status
- Organic vs paid preference
- What is most important to push this month
- What has already been used heavily
- What should not be repeated

## Content Production Constraints

Collect:

- Filming availability
- Design availability
- Editing availability
- AI generation suitability
- Asset availability
- Product photo availability
- Logo / packaging / brand assets
- Visual identity constraints
- People availability
- Approval bottlenecks
- Production limitations
