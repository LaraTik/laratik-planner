# Boosting and Paid Logic

Classify important content as:

- Organic
- Boost Candidate
- High-Priority Paid Creative

## For Any Boost / Paid Recommendation, Explain

- Why it is suitable
- What objective it supports
- What CTA it works with
- Whether it is better for awareness or conversion

## Boosting Preference

Suggest boosted reels especially when they have:

- strong visual hook
- broad relevance
- clear CTA
- clear conversion or awareness role
- strong audience fit
